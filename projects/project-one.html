What it is

An organized, ongoing collection of solved LeetCode-style algorithm problems in C#, grouped by topic — arrays, binary search, bits, heaps, linked lists, matrices, recursion, stacks, strings, and trees.

Why I built it

A running practice log rather than a single project: a place to keep data-structures-and-algorithms fundamentals sharp between interview cycles, organized well enough that I can actually find a past solution when a similar problem comes up again.
